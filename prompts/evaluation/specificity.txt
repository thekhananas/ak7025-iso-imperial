## Evaluation Criteria: SPECIFICITY
Does the feedback reference specific elements of the student's answer, or is it vague and generic?

Score 1: Entirely generic — could apply to any student's answer (e.g., "Good try, keep studying").
Score 2: Mostly generic with one vague reference to the student's actual content.
Score 3: Moderately specific — references some specific parts of the student's answer but also includes generic statements.
Score 4: Mostly specific — clearly references particular claims or phrases from the student's answer.
Score 5: Highly specific — directly quotes or paraphrases exact elements of the student's answer and ties feedback to those elements.

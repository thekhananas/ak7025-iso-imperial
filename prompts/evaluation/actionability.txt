## Evaluation Criteria: ACTIONABILITY
Does the feedback provide concrete, implementable suggestions the student can act on to improve?

Score 1: No actionable suggestions — only states what is right/wrong with no path forward.
Score 2: Vague suggestions (e.g., "review the material" or "study more").
Score 3: Some actionable advice but not clearly tied to the student's specific errors.
Score 4: Clear, actionable suggestions tied to specific gaps — student would know what to study next.
Score 5: Highly actionable — provides specific concepts to review, questions to consider, or steps to take, directly addressing the student's misconceptions.

## Evaluation Criteria: ACCURACY
Does the feedback correctly identify what the student got right and what they got wrong?

Score 1: Feedback contains factual errors or misidentifies correct parts as wrong (or vice versa).
Score 2: Feedback is mostly inaccurate — several key errors in identifying student strengths/weaknesses.
Score 3: Feedback is partially accurate — some correct identifications but notable misses or errors.
Score 4: Feedback is mostly accurate — correctly identifies most strengths and weaknesses with minor issues.
Score 5: Feedback is fully accurate — precisely identifies all correct and incorrect elements of the student's answer.
